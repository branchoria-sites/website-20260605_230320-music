## Introduction

For many unpaid or underpaid music creators, the problem is not that a song was streamed but that the systems handling the stream could not reliably identify who should be paid. Streaming services, labels, publishers, distributors and collecting societies all depend on metadata: the structured information that describes a recording, its songwriters, performers, rights holders and [ownership]({{ 'ownership/' | relative_url }}) shares. When that information is incomplete, inconsistent or missing, royalties can be delayed, placed into unmatched revenue pools or fail to reach creators altogether. The UK’s debate about streaming fairness therefore turned [attention]({{ 'attention/' | relative_url }}) not only to royalty rates but also to the quality of the data flowing through the music industry. Government-backed reforms have treated metadata as a practical mechanism for improving payment accuracy, transparency and creator recognition. <span class="citation-chip-wrap"><a class="citation-chip" href="https://www.simkins.com/news/music-industry-commits-to-addressing-metadata-streaming-and-fair-royalty-remuneration" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: simkins.com">[2simkins.com]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">simkins.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Music industry commits to addressing metadata, streaming...2 Jun 2023 — Addressing the perceived imbalance regarding music streaming roy...</span></span></span>

## What music metadata is supposed to capture

[Music]({{ 'music/' | relative_url }}) metadata is often described as the digital equivalent of album liner notes, but its role is much larger in the streaming era. It acts as the information infrastructure that connects a song to the people entitled to payment. Metadata can include:

* Song and [recording]({{ 'recording/' | relative_url }}) titles.
* Performer, producer and songwriter credits.
* Publisher and [rights]({{ 'rights/' | relative_url }})-holder information.
* Ownership splits between contributors. <span class="citation-chip-wrap"><a class="citation-chip" href="https://www.dataart.com/blog/who-owns-music-rights-understanding-music-ownership-with-ddex-by-russell-karp" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: dataart.com">[dataart.com]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">dataart.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">who owns music rights understanding music ownership with ddex by russell karp</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Understanding Music Ownership with DDEX20 Nov 2024 — DDEX (Digital Data Exchange) standards have emerged as a vital framework for facilit...</span></span></span>
* Industry identifiers such as ISRCs (recording identifiers), ISWCs (composition identifiers) and IPI numbers for writers.
* Territorial and licensing information. <span class="citation-chip-wrap"><a class="citation-chip" href="https://www.aulosa.com/post/music-metadata-explained" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: aulosa.com">[Aulosa]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">aulosa.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">What is Music Metadata?</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">AulosaWhat is Music Metadata?January 12, 2026 — 12 Jan 2026 — Music metadata is the structured information that identifies your recording...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: January 12, 2026</span></span></span> <span class="citation-chip-wrap"><a class="citation-chip" href="https://rightshub.net/news/uk-music-metadata-agreement-what-labels-need-to-know" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: rightshub.net">[2rightsHUB]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">rightshub.net</span><span class="citation-popover-title">uk music metadata agreement what labels need to know</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">The UK Music Metadata Agreement: What Independent...2 May 2026 — The UK Government has set a new standard for music streaming metadata...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: May 2026</span></span></span>

Streaming royalties move through multiple organisations before reaching creators. To make that journey work, the same identifying information must remain accurate across different databases and systems. Industry standards such as DDEX were developed to help labels, publishers, collecting societies and digital services exchange this information consistently. Without common data standards, every participant may hold slightly different information about the same recording, increasing the risk of payment errors. <span class="citation-chip-wrap"><a class="citation-chip" href="https://ddex.net/standards/recording-data-and-rights/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: ddex.net">[DDEX]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">ddex.net</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Recording Data and Rights</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">DDEXRecording Data and RightsMay 14, 2018 — The Recording Data and Rights Notification (RDR-N) standard enables the exchange of metadata...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: May 14, 2018</span></span></span>

The importance of metadata is easy to underestimate because listeners rarely see most of it. Yet behind every stream sits a chain of rights and payment calculations that depend on accurate contributor information. In practical terms, metadata is what tells the industry who made the music and who should receive money when it is used. <span class="citation-chip-wrap"><a class="citation-chip" href="https://sonosuite.com/blog/what-is-music-metadata-and-why-is-important-to-digital-music" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: sonosuite.com">[SonoSuite]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">sonosuite.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Why is it so important?Read more</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">SonoSuiteWhat is music metadata and why is important to digital musicLast but no least, accurate metadata has a key role in allocating ma...</span></span></span>

## How missing credits disrupt payment chains

The most direct consequence of poor metadata is that royalties cannot always be matched to the correct creator. A missing songwriter identifier, an incomplete ownership split or an incorrectly entered name can prevent automated systems from connecting usage data with the people entitled to payment. The UK Intellectual Property Office has explicitly noted that incomplete or inaccurate metadata can cause significant delays in payments and, in some cases, result in creators not being paid at all. <span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/uk-industry-agreement-on-music-streaming-metadata" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: GOV.UK">[GOV.UK]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">GOV.UK</span><span class="citation-popover-title">uk industry agreement on music streaming metadata</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">31 May 2023 — The UK industry agreement on music streaming metadata sets out a positive commitment from players across the music streamin...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: May 2023</span></span></span>

These failures often begin surprisingly early. Contributors may not agree [song splits]({{ 'song-splits/' | relative_url }}) at the point of creation. Different organisations may record names differently. Unique identifiers may never be assigned or may not be supplied when a recording is delivered to a distributor. Once errors enter the system, they can be copied and amplified as data moves through streaming services, publishers, collection societies and royalty processing systems. <span class="citation-chip-wrap"><a class="citation-chip" href="https://www.linkedin.com/posts/music-story_executive-summary-music-streaming-metadata-activity-7450445416204197888-OVDi" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: linkedin.com">[LinkedIn]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">linkedin.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Linked In Executive</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">LinkedInExecutive Summary: Music streaming metadata reportThe UK IPO&#x27;s latest report on music streaming metadata makes a critical point...</span></span></span>

**Cross-border complications.** Streaming is global, meaning metadata often has to travel across multiple territories and rights-management systems. ## The tiny errors that become big financial problems

Many metadata failures are not dramatic technical breakdowns. They are small administrative mistakes with cumulative effects.

A songwriter may be listed under a nickname rather than a registered professional name. A recording may be uploaded before composition information is completed. A contributor's identifier may be omitted from one database but included in another. Ownership percentages may not total correctly. Individually, these appear minor. Across millions of recordings and billions of streams, they create friction throughout the royalty system. <span class="citation-chip-wrap"><a class="citation-chip" href="https://www.linkedin.com/posts/music-story_executive-summary-music-streaming-metadata-activity-7450445416204197888-OVDi" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: linkedin.com">[LinkedIn]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">linkedin.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Linked In Executive</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">LinkedInExecutive Summary: Music streaming metadata reportThe UK IPO&#x27;s latest report on music streaming metadata makes a critical point...</span></span></span>

Researchers and industry groups have repeatedly highlighted that music metadata problems are rarely confined to a single point in the supply chain. Instead, errors emerge during creation, registration, distribution and rights administration, then compound as information is reused by different participants. The UK Industry Agreement on Music Streaming Metadata emerged from the wider policy debate about streaming fairness. ## Why metadata remains central to the fair-pay debate

Arguments about streaming remuneration often focus on how much money creators receive per stream. Metadata highlights a different question: can the industry reliably determine who should receive the money in the first place?

That distinction explains why metadata became one of the earliest areas where UK policymakers, industry bodies and creator organisations found common ground. Better metadata does not automatically increase earnings, but it can reduce avoidable payment failures, improve credit visibility and strengthen the ability of creators to claim what they are already owed. <span class="citation-chip-wrap"><a class="citation-chip" href="https://thefac.org/fac-guides-resources-directory/ipometadata" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: thefac.org">[Featured Artists Coalition]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">thefac.org</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Featured Artists CoalitionUnited Kingdom Industry Agreement on Music Streaming...2 Jun 2023 — The metadata code is a crucial first step...</span></span></span> <span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.gov.uk/guidance/uk-industry-agreement-on-music-streaming-metadata-working-groups" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: GOV.UK">[GOV.UK]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">GOV.UK</span><span class="citation-popover-title">uk industry agreement on music streaming metadata working groups</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Initial phase metadata KPI...Read more...</span></span></span>

    closeMenu(section);
  }
  sections.forEach(function (section) {
    if (section.getAttribute('data-ebay-localized-init') === '1') return;
    section.setAttribute('data-ebay-localized-init', '1');
    var available = availableMarkets(section);
    var marketId = defaultMarket;
    if (window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation && window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation.inferMarket) {
      marketId = window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation.inferMarket(available, {
        storageKey: 'phoenix-ebay-market',
        defaultMarket: defaultMarket
      });
    } else if (available.indexOf(defaultMarket) < 0) {
      marketId = available[0] || defaultMarket;
    }
    var select = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-select]');
    if (select) {
      select.addEventListener('change', function () {
        applyMarket(section, select.value, true);
      });
    }
    var trigger = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-trigger]');
    var menu = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-menu]');
    if (trigger && menu) {
      trigger.addEventListener('click', function (event) {
        event.stopPropagation();
        var expanded = trigger.getAttribute('aria-expanded') === 'true';
        menu.hidden = expanded;
        trigger.setAttribute('aria-expanded', expanded ? 'false' : 'true');
        var picker = trigger.closest('.fr-ebay-market-picker');
        if (picker) picker.classList.toggle('fr-ebay-market-picker--open', !expanded);
      });
      Array.prototype.slice.call(menu.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-option]')).forEach(function (button) {
        button.addEventListener('click', function (event) {
          event.stopPropagation();
          applyMarket(section, button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-option'), true);
        });
      });
      document.addEventListener('click', function () { closeMenu(section); });
    }
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]')).forEach(function (button) {
      button.addEventListener('click', function () {
        applyMarket(section, button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button'), true);
      });
    });
    applyMarket(section, marketId, false);
  });
})();
